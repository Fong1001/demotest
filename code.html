From Intake to Delivery

A meticulous 7-step process ensuring industrial-scale precision and artisanal quality for every piece of timber.

1

Log Selection

Sourcing premium raw materials from sustainable forests. Each log undergoes strict evaluation for grain structure, density, and potential yield.

2

Cutting & Sizing

Industrial-scale bandsaws and precision CNC machinery execute rough cuts and precise sizing according to client specifications with minimal waste.

3

Processing

Kiln drying and moisture equalization. We meticulously control the environment to ensure optimal stability and prevent future warping or splitting.

4

Treatment

Application of industrial-grade preservatives and protective coatings to enhance durability against environmental factors and biological degradation.

5

Assembly

For specialized orders, components are joined and assembled using industrial adhesives and structural fasteners to create complex timber products.

6

Quality Check

Rigorous manual and automated inspections. We verify dimensions, structural integrity, and finish quality against our stringent 30-year standards.

7

Delivery

Secure packaging and logistics coordination. We manage industrial-scale shipping to ensure products arrive safely and on schedule, globally.
